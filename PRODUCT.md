# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Vite + React 18 + TypeScript + Tailwind 3, deployed on Vercel (bhuvana-resume.vercel.app) with @vercel/analytics. Single route (`/`) plus a NotFound page.

## Purpose

Personal portfolio of Bhuvana Sundaram, a frontend-focused full-stack engineer (8+ years) based in Bengaluru, India. It exists to get her hired into senior/staff frontend roles.

## Users

Primary: hiring managers and recruiters for senior/staff frontend roles, skimming quickly between other candidates, usually on a laptop. They need to see within seconds: who she is, the level she works at (first UI hire and lead UI developer at Aerospike, architecture, component library, DBaaS console, Voyager desktop app), and proof she ships.
Secondary: engineers on the hiring loop who click through to projects and GitHub.
